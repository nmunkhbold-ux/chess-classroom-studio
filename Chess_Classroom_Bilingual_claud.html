Welcome to Chess Classroom

Use the Teaching tools panel in fullscreen (press M to hide/show). Choose Full game for regular chess or Piece lesson to teach how one selected piece moves. In a piece lesson you can show possible destinations, add blockers, and demonstrate captures. Click or drag a piece to move it.
